About

          

            
I'm an Information Technology Specialist and a Cum Laude graduate of Batangas State University TNEU — Lipa Campus with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology major in Networking Technology.

            
I have experience creating and sustaining scalable web application backend systems with a focus on performance, security, and reliability. I've modeled and developed RESTful APIs, processed server-side logic, and maintained database designs. I work closely with front-end developers and product teams to turn requirements into effective technical solutions.

            
I'm passionate about IoT, AI-driven applications, and cloud technologies—with hands-on experience in AWS, Java EE, and containerization. I bring strong communication skills, adaptability, and project management to what I do.
